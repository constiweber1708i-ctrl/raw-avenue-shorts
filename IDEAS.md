# Topic ideas backlog

Candidate myths for future calendar slots. None of these is verified yet: before a topic is used, the
run must confirm the reversal in at least two independent sources it has opened (drop it otherwise and
note why below). Take ideas from the top; when fewer than 10 remain, research and add new ones.

## Rules for a good topic
- A belief most people hold + a reversal you can prove with a date, number, name or document.
- Enough story for two parts: Part 1 = the myth and why it's wrong; Part 2 = where the myth came from.
- Visual in stick figures (people, objects, places), no gore or graphic violence.
- Spread across eras and regions: no more than two topics in a row from the same century or country.
- Avoid: current politics, religious disputes, ethnicity debates, living people, tragedies with living
  survivors, medical advice.
- Never repeat a topic already in `topics/` or `calendar.json`.
